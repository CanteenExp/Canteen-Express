One accent (orange) is reserved for the single primary action per screen and the active nav item. Green, amber, blue and red appear only when they mean a status. Everything else is neutral, separated by 1px borders rather than shadows.

Type

One family, four sizes, two weights. Labels are sentence case — never ALL CAPS.

Page title — 22 / 600

Section title — 16 / 600

Body — 14 / 400. The default for text, table cells and control labels.

Caption — 12 / 400. Column headers, hints, helper lines.

Family: Plus Jakarta Sans, already self-hosted in the repo as a variable font, so 400 and 600 cost one request.

The rules this system encodes

One primary action

Orange marks the single most important action on a screen and the active nav item. If two things compete, one of them is secondary.

Colour means something

Green, amber, blue and red are status only. Nothing is tinted for decoration.

Borders, not shadows

Depth comes from 1px hairlines and spacing. The one soft shadow is for modals and menus.

Thumb first

Every control is at least 44px, and every icon is the same 20px at a 1.75 stroke.
