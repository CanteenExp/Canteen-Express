2. Payment and Claiming: Pay at the designated cashier counter by presenting your queue number or order slip. Check the completeness of your order before leaving the claiming area — concerns raised afterwards will not be honored.

4. Menu Availability: All items are subject to stock on hand. If an item becomes unavailable before payment, you may choose an alternate, modify your order, or cancel the affected item.
